# Safal Vidyalaya — Official Photography & Video Checklist

> **Purpose:** This document specifies all photos, videos, and media assets required from the school administration to finalize and launch the official Safal Vidyalaya website.

---

## 📸 Photography Guidelines for the School Media Team

1. **Orientation:** Take all building, classroom, and campus photos in **Landscape mode (horizontal, 16:9 ratio)**.
2. **Resolution:** Minimum **1920 × 1080 px** (High-Resolution JPEG or RAW preferred).
3. **Lighting & Clarity:** Shoot during morning or bright daytime hours with natural sunlight. Avoid blurry phone zooms or dim indoor lighting.
4. **Subjects:** Students must be in neat, clean school uniforms, actively engaged (reading, experimenting, smiling, or working on projects) rather than staring stiffly at the camera.
